\begin{abstract}
Daily EUR/USD returns are fat-tailed. Using \result{data.n_days} daily ECB reference
rates from \result{data.start} to \result{data.end}, the excess kurtosis of log returns is
\result{h1.kurtosis} (95\,\% block-bootstrap interval \result{h1.kurtosis_lo} to
\result{h1.kurtosis_hi}). Moves larger than three standard deviations occur on
\result{h2.share} of days, \result{h2.ratio} times as often as a normal distribution
implies. Both results were pre-registered.
\end{abstract}
