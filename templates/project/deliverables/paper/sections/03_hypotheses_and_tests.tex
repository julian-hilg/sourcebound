\section{Hypotheses and tests}\label{sec:hypotheses}

\textbf{H1} states that daily returns are fat-tailed. The test is the sample excess
kurtosis with a moving-block bootstrap interval; H1 is supported if the lower bound is
positive. \textbf{H2} states that a normal model understates large moves. The test counts
days with standardised returns beyond three standard deviations against the normal
probability with a one-sided binomial test at the 1\,\% level.
