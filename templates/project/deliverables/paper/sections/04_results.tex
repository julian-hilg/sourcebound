\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

The excess kurtosis is \result{h1.kurtosis} with a 95\,\% interval from
\result{h1.kurtosis_lo} to \result{h1.kurtosis_hi}; H1 is supported. Large moves are
frequent: \result{h2.count} days exceed three standard deviations, a share of
\result{h2.share} against \result{h2.share_normal} under normality ($p =
\result{h2.pvalue}$); H2 is supported. \Cref{fig:tails} shows that the gap widens
further out in the tail, and \cref{tab:tails} gives the counts.
