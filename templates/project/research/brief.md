# Brief (Gate G1)

**Question.** Are daily EUR/USD returns fat-tailed, and by how much does a normal
distribution understate the probability of large daily moves?

**Why it matters.** Risk limits, option prices and stress tests that assume normal returns
understate tail risk. The size of the understatement is what a practitioner needs.

**Deliverables.**
1. Working paper (English, `sbpaper`), about 6 pages.
2. Public repository with code, results and data manifest.

**Constraints.** Free data only (ECB reference rates). Two days of work.

**Out of scope.** Intraday data, volatility forecasting models, other currency pairs
except as robustness.

**Success criterion.** A pre-registered test with a clear decision and an effect size in
units a reader can use (probability of a 3-sigma day).
